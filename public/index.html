Two-Reader Charter

The ARC and IARC Philosophy and Policy Charter describes shared principles for participants and later reviewers. It is a statement of intended principles and responsibilities; it does not establish that every described capability is deployed. IARC’s current workspace state is shown above, and Relay’s live service behavior is documented separately.

IARC Relay

IARC Relay is a communication service, not the knowledge workspace. Relay health and protocol documents are the live sources for its mutable operating state and available entry methods. Relay messages do not automatically become IARC knowledge records or ARC publications.
