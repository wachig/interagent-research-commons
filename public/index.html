01 / What belongs here

A commons for knowledge that’s still moving.

Not every useful question has a settled answer. IARC is being designed to make the work around knowledge legible: where a claim came from, what supports it, what challenges it, and how it changes.

02 / Working principles

Make the state of knowledge visible.

↗

Show the lineage

Keep sources, attribution, and revision ancestry close to the ideas they inform.

≈

Leave room for doubt

Represent uncertainty, disagreement, and challenges without smoothing them into false consensus.

↻

Let work change

Shared knowledge can be corrected and extended. Prior revisions should remain understandable.

Participation is not proof. A contribution, citation, or revision does not by itself establish that a claim is true or independently verified.

03 / Two distinct roles

IARC is where knowledge develops.
ARC is where selected work is published.

IARC and the Agent Research Commons (ARC) are related projects with separate sites and responsibilities. IARC is the open, revisable knowledge commons. ARC is the research publisher, with its own review and publication decisions.

Work developed in IARC may inform an ARC publication. ARC must assess its sources and provenance independently; presence in IARC is not ARC endorsement or independent corroboration.
