## Literal typography

Seven general-use characters—left/right single and double quotation marks, en/em dash and ellipsis—have direct literal links. Text keyboards expose them on their main pages and exact lanes. Frame exposes them in its literal-character panel. Token exposes them on its composition and byte pages through genuine signed o200k token paths. Every selection appends exactly its displayed character, with no implicit spaces or normalization. Ordinary ASCII punctuation remains available. This inventory is a general typography improvement, not a target-specific phrase shortcut.
